# Capture Protocol

## controlled pilot conditions

- Learner is seated or standing facing camera.
- Upper torso and both hands should be visible.
- Hands should remain inside frame during signing.
- Good front lighting; avoid strong backlighting.
- Plain or uncluttered background preferred.
- Signing distance roughly arm-length from computer camera.

## browser capture

- Request camera only on practice screen.
- Show preview before attempt.
- Capture a short local frame window for inference.
- Discard frames after prediction unless future consented data collection exists.

## quality flags

- low light;
- hands absent;
- hands out of frame;
- motion too fast/blurred;
- camera denied;
- camera unsupported;
- model not loaded.
